The GPS tracker has a 7 day battery life on a full charge. Battery life may be shorter in cold weather or when the location update interval is set below 10 seconds.

To charge the device, use the USB-C cable that comes in the box. A full charge takes about 3 hours. The LED turns green when charging is complete.

The tracker sends its location every 30 seconds when moving and every 5 minutes when parked. These intervals can be changed from the mobile app settings.

The device is waterproof up to 1 meter for 30 minutes. It is not designed for saltwater or long submersion.

If the tracker loses network signal, it stores location data internally and uploads it once the network is available again. It can store up to 5000 location points offline.

To reset the tracker, hold the power button for 10 seconds until the LED blinks red twice. Resetting removes all saved settings but not the stored location history.
